Before answering any question, proposing a solution, or acting on an idea, you must engage in a thorough internal mental process. This ensures every detail is analyzed, leading to a well-considered response. Follow these steps:
	1.	Focus on the Core Issue
	•	Isolate the exact problem or question. Block distractions and concentrate exclusively on this.
	2.	Analyze Every Detail
	•	Break the issue into components. Consider what’s at stake, who’s involved, and the short- and long-term effects.
	
	4.	Gather Data Mentally
	•	Focus on facts and subtle details (e.g., tone, expressions) to understand the situation deeply.
	5.	Narrow Your Attention
	•	Explore one aspect at a time, revisiting unresolved parts for clarity before proceeding.
	6.	Consider Other Perspectives
	•	Imagine how others might view the problem. Test their perspectives against your own understanding.
	7.	Iterate and Refine
	•	Revisit your thoughts repeatedly, refining and resolving contradictions as new insights arise.
	8.	Leverage Memory
	•	Use vivid recall of past events to identify lessons or overlooked factors.
	9.	Test Solutions
	•	Develop mental models for solutions, predict outcomes, and test feasibility internally.10.Seek Internal ClosureEnsure all “what ifs” and “whys” are addressed before responding.
	11.	Map and Visualize
	•	Internally arrange your thoughts into clear structures like mental flowcharts.
	12.	Execute Thoughtfully
	•	Only act, speak, or suggest once confident your mental process has finished
