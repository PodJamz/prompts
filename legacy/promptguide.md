## **🎯 Prompting Methodology Overview**

**A good prompting method has four core components:**

* **Context:** Clearly framing the background.  
* **Intention:** Precisely defining your desired outcome.  
* **Constraints:** Outlining limitations or specific requirements.  
* **Follow-up:** Encouraging iterative refinement and clarity.

Here’s how you can systematically apply this structure:

---

## **🧠 1\. Establish Clear Context**

*Context grounds your prompt and makes it specific.*

* **Define clearly what's happening:**  
  * Project phase (Planning, Coding, Debugging, Optimization).  
  * Specific technologies or frameworks involved.  
  * Current issues or challenges.

**Example:**

"I'm optimizing an asyncio-based Python TTS service (`InterruptibleTTSService`) that processes audio chunks incrementally. Currently, it suffers from playback choppiness."

---

## **🎯 2\. State Your Intention Clearly**

*Express precisely what you want as a result.*

* **Ask directly:**  
  * "Suggest improvements..."  
  * "Review this implementation for bottlenecks..."  
  * "Propose an incremental approach to solve X..."

**Example:**

"I’d like help identifying the cause of choppy playback and implementing changes that ensure smooth audio streaming."

---

## **🚧 3\. Provide Relevant Constraints and Requirements**

*Constraints shape realistic solutions.*

* **Mention explicitly:**  
  * Technical constraints (e.g., no file I/O, memory limits, latency requirements).  
  * Minimal changes or no major rewrites.  
  * Any external dependencies or standards you must adhere to.

**Example:**

"Solutions must preserve the current interrupt responsiveness and avoid significant structural changes. Ideally, minimal changes to existing async logic."

---

## **🔄 4\. Plan for Follow-Up Iterations**

*Effective prompts anticipate iterative improvement.*

* **Always finish with:**  
  * "If this isn't clear, let me know and I’ll provide more details."  
  * "Do you recommend additional areas I should consider?"  
  * "Would you prefer further clarification or examples?"

**Example:**

"After implementing your suggestions, could you help me plan a benchmarking session to measure improvements accurately?"

---

## **✅ Final Example of a Great Prompt (Full Cycle):**

**Context:**  
 "My Python-based TTS service using asyncio and incremental audio chunk processing is experiencing playback jitter and occasional dropouts."

**Intention:**  
 "I'd like your help diagnosing potential causes of these performance issues and implementing targeted fixes to smooth audio output."

**Constraints:**  
 "Please prioritize minimal code changes, preserve current interrupt responsiveness, and avoid introducing new external dependencies or excessive complexity."

**Follow-Up:**  
 "If more technical context or details about the audio processing pipeline are needed, please let me know. After adjustments, I'd also appreciate your guidance on setting up effective tests to verify the improvements."

---

## **🗒️ Quick Prompting Checklist**

* Have I clearly provided context?  
* Is my intended outcome clear and direct?  
* Have I stated important constraints explicitly?  
* Am I leaving space for iterative follow-up?
